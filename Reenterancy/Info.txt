What is Reentrancy?

Reentrancy happens when:

Contract A sends ETH to Contract B.
Before Contract A finishes its execution, Contract B calls back into Contract A.
Contract A's state has not been updated yet.
The attacker repeats the process and drains funds.

Think of it like:

Bank gives you money before updating your balance.

While the bank is still processing, you ask for another withdrawal.

The bank still thinks you have the original balance
